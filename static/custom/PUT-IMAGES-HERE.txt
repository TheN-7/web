Drop your own images in this folder. They are picked up automatically
(no restart needed). Refresh the page to see them.

  background.jpg   (or .jpeg / .png / .webp / .gif)
      Full-screen background behind the glass panels.
      Tip: a large image, e.g. 1920x1080 or bigger, under ~1 MB loads fastest.

  logo.png         (or .svg / .webp / .jpg)
      Replaces the folder icon in the top bar and on the login screen.
      A square image works best.

  favicon.ico      (or .png / .svg)
      The little icon in the browser tab. A square PNG of 64x64 or larger
      works fine. If there's no favicon but there is a logo, the logo is used.

Delete or rename the file to go back to the default look.
